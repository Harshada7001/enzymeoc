Organizational Challenges

Lack of Employee Feedback

Companies that do not provide regular feedback to employees experience higher turnover rates. According to Gallup, organizations that implement regular feedback have turnover rates that are 14.9% lower than those that do not.

Communication Gap

There is often a significant gap between how managers and employees perceive performance management. While 71% of managers believe they provide clear directions, only 44% of employees agree, as per Gallup.

Outdated PMS Tools

Many companies in India are reevaluating their current performance management systems, indicating a need for modernization in this area, according to Deloitte.



Low Employee Engagement

Organizations with disengaged employees face challenges in productivity and profitability. Gallup reports that highly engaged employees can lead to a 22% increase in profitability and a 21% increase in productivity.

Lack of Real-time Feedback

Employees prefer real-time feedback over annual reviews. According to PwC, 75% of employees believe feedback should be given in real-time.



Underutilization Technology

Companies that do not leverage technology for performance management miss out on productivity gains. McKinsey reports that advanced analytics in HR can increase productivity by 10-15%.

Low Market Valuation

Ineffective performance management can lead to a 16% decrease in market valuation, according to the Boston Consulting Group.
